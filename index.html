Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you visit.

Are the displayed BDT prices official Bangladesh retail prices?

No. They are illustrative conversions from publicly accessible third-party INR listings at the demo rate shown on the website. Final Bangladesh price, promotion and availability must be confirmed by the showroom.

Can I place and pay for an order on this website?

No. This independent prototype does not collect real payments or submit confirmed orders. Use the fabric list as a planning or quotation aid, then contact a showroom.

How much fabric will I need?

The amount depends on garment type, measurements, fabric width, pattern matching and the tailor’s cutting plan. Calculator results are estimates; confirm the final quantity with your tailor or showroom specialist.

Is every fabric available at every showroom?

Branch-level stock is not published in this prototype. Call your chosen showroom with the product name or code before visiting.

What are the delivery area, fee and timeline?

The public Raymond Bangladesh website currently marks delivery policy as under development. Confirm coverage, fee, timeline, courier method and payment terms directly before purchase.

Can tailored or altered items be returned?

Public guidance distinguishes ready-to-wear from customized clothing and notes that customized products are generally non-returnable. The exact decision depends on purchase channel and approved terms.

Where do the catalogue images and specifications come from?

The private prototype uses publicly accessible third-party Raymond listings for illustration, with source links and disclaimers. It is not an official Raymond or ManTire catalogue.

  • Confirm whether delivery is available for your address.
  • Confirm the delivery fee and expected timeline.
  • Confirm product availability and the final payable amount.

Purchase-channel specific

Exchange & returns

Terms depend on what you buy.

Public guidance states that return and exchange conditions vary by purchase location. Ready-to-wear and custom-tailored purchases are treated differently, and customized products are generally non-returnable.

  • Confirm eligibility and the allowed time window before paying.
  • Retain the receipt, tags, packaging and any written terms.
  • Inspect fabric, colour, quantity and workmanship promptly.
  • Report a concern to the original showroom without delay.
